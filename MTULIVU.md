## The stance

Mtulivu is not a meditation brand and not a job title.

It is the working posture:

1. Stay composed.
2. Invent something with a real name (kcal.lol, not “Project Phoenix”).
3. Build it.
4. Architect the cloud it stands on.
5. Take it all the way to production.
